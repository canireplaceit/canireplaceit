Can I replace Notion?

You can almost replace Notion: Joplin covers most of it under AGPL-3.0. What you give up is databases and relations.

Databases and relations are the moat. Editing and wikis port fine; the “Notion as an app platform” part does not.

proposal document

Proposals

Three structurally different directions in one file — switch skins bottom-right and re-walk any page. What changes per skin: the nav (top bar / left sidebar / masthead), the hero (headline / terminal window / account book), the list rendering (cards / dense rows / journal lines), the ad chrome (rails / bracket boxes / classifieds).

1 · The verdict becomes physical motion

A stamp that slams in on product pages (click it to replay). Verdict color drives card edges, chips, rung bars — brand blue is never a verdict.

2 · Exit ladder drawn as a descent component

You-are-here at top, each rung animates in with its yearly saving. Turns “12 alternatives” into a decision path.

3 · “Your bill” page new page

Tick your SaaS, watch the year total in red, then the ladder crosses lines out. Working demo in this file. The ledger hero is the same idea as brand imagery.

4 · Command palette front door new

⌘K everywhere; the answer (verdict + price + top escape) appears in the dropdown itself, before any page loads.

5 · Graveyard as a destination new

Tombstones with epitaphs generated from health data. Screenshot bait, and the most honest page on the internet about OSS churn.

6 · Sponsor chrome consolidated fix

Today the real site runs rails + marquee tape + a 10-cell wall on every page. Keep the wall on home, rails elsewhere, kill the tape. Fewer, calmer slots sell better. Open slots stay visible — they are the rate card in situ (see the dashed units all over this mock).

7 · Feature-fit % on every alternative new

The matrix already knows it. One number for skimmers; the matrix stays for the careful.

8 · Numbers behave like telemetry motion

Count-ups on stats, +1 tick on “I switched”, the bleed counter on receipts, tabular numerals everywhere.

9 · Receipts look like receipts tweak

Perforated edge, dashed rules, mono totals, confidence in color.

10 · Matrix crosshair motion

Column highlight follows the cursor; sticky first column; “you” column tinted.

11 · Fix the AA contrast bug fix

#2f6fed is 4.34:1 on light backgrounds — body links must use the darker sibling (#0e64e0 per DESIGN-GUIDELINES.md). Applied in this file as --accent-text.

12 · Motion rules rules

≤500ms, ease-out entries, one orchestrated moment per page, hovers move ≤4px, prefers-reduced-motion kills everything (wired here).
